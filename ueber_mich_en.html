About Me

I am a graduate in German Studies and hold an M.A. in Foreign Language Linguistics, specializing in German as a Foreign Language and Language Acquisition and Communication. For over 15 years, I have worked in language education, communication, and intercultural education – both in Germany and internationally.

My professional experience includes:

  • German as a Foreign and Second Language (DaF/DaZ)
  • Integration and business language courses
  • Medical German & Specialist Language Exam (FSP)
  • Communication training
  • Intercultural education
  • Democracy promotion as well as radicalization and extremism prevention

I have worked at educational institutions and projects in Aschaffenburg, Frankfurt am Main, Potsdam, and Berlin, and have experience collaborating with international professionals, companies, doctors, and institutions.

My teaching and training combine professional quality, intercultural sensitivity, and practical communication. I place particular emphasis on a professional, motivating, and appreciative learning atmosphere.
