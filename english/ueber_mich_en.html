About Me

I hold a degree in German studies and an M.A. in foreign language linguistics with a focus on German as a foreign language as well as language acquisition and communication. For over 15 years, I have worked in language education, communication, and intercultural education - both in Germany and internationally.

My professional experience includes:

  • German as a Foreign and Second Language (DaF/DaZ)
  • Integration and vocational language courses
  • Medical German & Professional Language Exam (FSP)
  • Communication training
  • Intercultural educational work
  • Democracy education and prevention of radicalization and extremism

I have worked at educational institutions and projects in Aschaffenburg, Frankfurt am Main, Potsdam, and Berlin, and I have experience collaborating with international professionals, companies, physicians, and institutions.

My teaching and training combine professional quality, intercultural sensitivity, and practical communication. I place special emphasis on a professional, motivating, and appreciative learning atmosphere.
